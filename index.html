Plant Care Essentials

Three golden rules to keep your green companions healthy.

1. Light over Water

Most indoor plants prefer medium, indirect sun. Avoid intense direct afternoon rays that scorch foliage.

2. Check the Soil

Poke a finger 2 inches into the soil. If it feels completely dry, give it a deep drink. If damp, wait 2–3 days.

3. Drainage is Vital

Ensure your planters have bottom drainage holes so roots never sit in standing water.
